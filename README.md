# Hey there! 👋

I'm **Lukáš Picek**, a computer vision and applied AI researcher and PI at the [University of West Bohemia](https://www.zcu.cz/en/) 🇨🇿.

My research sits at the intersection of **computer vision, machine learning, and biodiversity science**. I develop methods, datasets, benchmarks, and open-source tools to help us better understand the natural world, from identifying individual animals and recognizing species to predicting where they occur.

Over the past three years, I've had the opportunity to work with some great people at **[Inria](https://www.inria.fr/en)** 🇫🇷, as part of the [Pl@ntNet](https://plantnet.org/) team, and **[MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/)** 🇺🇸, where I was a Fulbright Visiting Scholar in the [Beery Lab](https://beerylab.csail.mit.edu/).

I particularly enjoy collaborating with ecologists, biologists, and other domain experts to tackle scientific challenges where AI can make a difference. I'm also interested in what happens **beyond the benchmark**: whether our models generalize, how their errors affect scientific conclusions, and how to turn research into tools that others can actually use.

## 🔬 What I'm working on

- 🧪 **Trustworthy Benchmarks & Evaluation** — Developing datasets and evaluation protocols that reveal model limitations, assess generalization, and measure performance beyond conventions.
- 🐾 **Animal re-identification & population monitoring** — Recognizing individual animals and connecting AI with ecological research.
- 🍄 **Fine-grained species recognition** — Identifying plants, fungi, and animals in challenging long-tailed and open-world settings.
- 🌍 **Species distribution modeling** — Combining biodiversity data with environmental and spatial data to predict species distributions.
